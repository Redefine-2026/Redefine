THE BRIEF

Take something familiar. Question everything about it.

Redefine is a 24-hour designathon where creativity meets innovation, challenging participants to rethink the digital experiences they interact with every day.

Rather than improving what already exists, you’ll question established design patterns, challenge assumptions, and reimagine how technology can serve people — working across user experience, visual design and interaction design to turn an unconventional idea into something intuitive, engaging and future-ready. There are no fixed answers here. Take something familiar, question everything about it, and build something better.

JUDGING

Four things the judges score

Craft carries as much weight as the idea. A radical concept in careless type and colour won’t clear the bar — and neither will a beautiful screen that changes nothing.

01
Criterion

Architecture

How the experience is structured — information hierarchy, navigation, and whether the flow holds up under real use.

02
Criterion

Colour Palette

Deliberate colour with a working system behind it: contrast, accessibility, and a palette chosen for this product.

03
Criterion

Typography

Type as a decision, not a default. Scale, pairing, weight, spacing, and readability across the whole interface.

04
Criterion

Creativity

How far you moved from the original. The bolder the departure — and the better you justify it — the higher this scores.

FAQ

Before you ask

Anything not covered here goes out to registered teams by email before the 16th.

What does it cost?

₹150 per ticket, plus GST. That’s per person, and it covers the full 24 hours at the venue.

Can I enter on my own?

Yes. Teams are 1 to 4 members, so a solo entry is a valid team.

Is this a coding event?

It’s a designathon. The judging criteria are architecture, colour palette, typography and creativity — so the work is judged as design. How far you take it into a working prototype is up to you and your team.

What will I be designing?

A set of tracks is announced at the start of the event, and your team chooses one of them to work in. Expect familiar territory — music and streaming, e-commerce, social platforms, productivity tools, entertainment, everyday apps. Whichever track you pick, the rule is the same: you’re replacing something familiar, not polishing it.

Can I start designing before the event?

The brief drops at 08:00 on 16 September and the clock starts there. Coming in with sharpened tools, a set-up file and a point of view is fine; coming in with the deliverable is not.

Where exactly is it?

Sarojini Naidu Gallery, from 08:00 AM on 16 September to 08:00 AM on 17 September. Reporting time and what to bring will be sent to registered teams.

Is OD provided?

Yes. OD is provided for the full duration of the event, from 08:00 AM on 16 September to 08:00 AM on 17 September.
